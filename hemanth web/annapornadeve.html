Understanding Food Waste in the World

Food waste occurs across farms, factories, shops, and homes, and contributes 8–10% of global greenhouse gas emissions. Reducing waste helps both people and planet. [web:36]

Global Food Waste Snapshot

1.05B tons of food wasted annually worldwide.

60% of waste comes from households.

Recovering just 25% could end global hunger. [web:45]

Food Waste by Sector

Households: Major source of waste due to overbuying and poor storage.
Food Service: Restaurants, hotels, canteens discarding leftovers.
Retail: Strict cosmetic standards and “best-before” confusion.
Farms & Processing: Harvest losses, grading, and logistics issues.

Most Wasted Food Types

Fruits & Vegetables: High spoilage rates.
Bread & Cereals: Commonly thrown away at home.
Dairy & Eggs: Sensitive to date labels.
Prepared Meals: Leftovers and buffet waste.
