Why Annapoorna Seva?

A simple web platform where anyone can pledge extra food, schedule pickups, and track their social impact.

Easy To Use

Fill a small form and our volunteers coordinate pickup or serving.

Safe & Hygienic

Basic checks ensure that only fresh, safely handled food is shared.

Community Impact

Your donations support nearby shelters, slums, and roadside workers.

How It Works

1. Pledge

Use the Donate page to share details of surplus food or sponsor meals.

2. Match

Volunteers map donations to nearby communities and NGOs.

3. Serve

Food is delivered or served, and your impact is counted in meals.
